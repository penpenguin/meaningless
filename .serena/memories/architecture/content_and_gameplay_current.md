Content/gameplay architecture summary compressed from older dated memories:

- Content registry: fish and decor definitions live under `src/content/`; fish are one module per species in `src/content/fish/*.ts`, decor in `src/content/decor/*.ts`, and `src/content/registry.ts` validates duplicate IDs and exposes getters/list/starter selectors. Legacy selectors in `src/game/catalog.ts` and `src/utils/speciesCatalog.ts` are compatibility layers backed by the registry. Add a fish by creating its fish module and exporting it from `src/content/fish/index.ts`.
- Observation unlocks: game profile stats include `totalViewedSeconds`; migration/default handling is in `src/game/gameSave.ts` and `src/game/types.ts`. `src/game/unlocks.ts` calculates observed seconds and remaining watch-time requirements. `src/game/gameReducer.ts` gates fish unlocks on observation time, increments viewed time only on visible `GAME/TICK`, preserves offline totals during visible play, and avoids new offline summaries during regular ticks. `GameHudOverlay` shows fish descriptions, observation progress, remaining time, and progress cards.
- Render model/gameplay cues: `createAquariumRenderModel` derives fish school mood (`calm|alert|feeding`), depth variance, and turn bias from water quality, comfort, crowding, and lane density. `simulation.ts` applies lane-imbalance penalties only once total fish reaches 15+ and uses decor hideout scoring to offset crowding.
